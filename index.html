Privacy Policy

At Iaat Country Club, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or make a reservation.

1. Information We Collect: We may collect personal details such as your full name, phone number, email address, and booking preferences when you register or reserve a service.

2. Use of Information: Your information is used strictly to process bookings, communicate updates regarding your reservations, and improve our club services.

3. Data Security: We implement robust security measures via secure cloud services (Firebase) to keep your personal records safe from unauthorized access.

Welcome to Iaat Country Club. By accessing our platform and booking our services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions:

1. Reservations & Bookings: All bookings made through our system are subject to availability and confirmation. Please ensure your contact details are accurate.
